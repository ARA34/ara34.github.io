Recent News

  • September 2026: I transitioned to a year-round internship with the Secure Algorithms team at Sandia Labs!
  • May - August 2026: I joined Sandia National Laboratories as an intern in the National Security Programs and worked with the Computer Science Research Institute supporting AI/ML projects part of the Genesis Mission.

I am a first-generation undergraduate computer science student in the CS Theory Group, mentored by the very thoughtful Vatsal Sharan and members of his group. Before USC, I had a great time working with Clare Baek, Dana Saito-Stehberger, and Mark Warschauer in the Digital Learning Lab at the University of California, Irvine.

My interests span trustworthy machine learning from the perspective of optimization, security, and learning dynamics. I research the behavior of machine learning algorithms when they are in adversarial, noise-prone, and resource-limited environments. My work is motivated by the goal of using tools in computer science for developing solutions to societal problems. See brief descriptions of my past projects here.

I actively participate in student-led organizations at USC as the president of SACNAS, and once Programming Chair for Color Stack as well as a Committee Director for SHPE.
